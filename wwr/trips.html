Our Signature Trips

Half-Day Splash

Perfect for first-timers and families, the Half-Day Splash covers eight miles of playful class II–III rapids. You'll get a full safety briefing, a splash-happy ride through Bailey's Bar, and plenty of calm water to soak in the canyon scenery.

  • 3 Hours
  • Class II–III
  • Ages 6+

Full-Day Expedition

Our most popular trip runs sixteen miles through the heart of the canyon, including the legendary Los Dientes del Diablo rapid. A riverside lunch is included, and experienced guides adjust the pace to match your crew's confidence on the water.

  • 7 Hours
  • Class III–IV
  • Ages 12+

Overnight Basecamp

Raft by day and camp under the stars by night. This two-day expedition includes all camping gear, riverside meals, and a run through Wild Sheep Rapid before a relaxed second day back to the take-out point.

  • 2 Days
  • Class III–V
  • Ages 14+
