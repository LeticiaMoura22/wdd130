Wild River Adventures

At Wild River Adventures, we believe every rapid tells a story. Our expert guides lead thrilling, safe, and unforgettable journeys down the region's most exciting rivers. Adventure isn't just what we do — it's who we are.

History

Wild River Adventures was founded in 1998 by a small group of river guides who wanted to share their love of the outdoors with the world. What started as a single raft and a handful of loyal customers has grown into one of the region's most trusted white water rafting outfitters, guiding thousands of adventurers safely down the rapids every year.
